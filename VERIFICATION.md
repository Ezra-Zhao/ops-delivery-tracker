## 5. Known limits (stated, not hidden)

- Demo scale and synthetic throughout; no auth, access control, audit log,
  concurrency, or notification features.
- Ticketing and reporting integrations are design assumptions only
  (docs/03-integration-diagram.md).
- Quantity forecasting from the JD is represented only at demo scale
  (per-site milestone counts and a forecast-dated milestone set); a
  capacity-quantity forecast model is not implemented and is not claimed.
- Local repository only: git-initialised, no remote, nothing pushed.
